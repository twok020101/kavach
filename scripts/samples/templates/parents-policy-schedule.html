Policy Wording (extract)

Section 3. Hospitalisation expenses

3.1 Room rent. The Company shall pay Room, Boarding and Nursing expenses as provided by the Hospital, up to 1% of the Sum Insured per day, or the actual charges, whichever is lower. The eligible room category is a single private room with rent up to this limit.

3.2 Proportionate deduction. If the Insured Person is admitted in a room category with a rent per day higher than the eligible limit specified in the Schedule, the Company shall reduce the amount payable for Associated Medical Expenses in the same proportion as the eligible rate per day bears to the actual rate per day of the room charges. Associated Medical Expenses shall not include (i) cost of pharmacy and consumables, (ii) cost of implants and medical devices, and (iii) cost of diagnostics. Proportionate deduction shall not apply in respect of Hospitals where differential billing is not followed based on room category, or in respect of expenses that are not differentially charged based on room category.

3.3 Intensive Care Unit. The Company shall pay ICU / ICCU expenses as billed by the Hospital. Proportionate deductions shall not be applied in respect of ICU charges, as ICU charges do not differ by room category.

3.4 Pre- and post-hospitalisation. Medical expenses incurred up to 30 days before admission and up to 60 days after discharge, for the same condition, are payable if the in-patient claim is admissible.

3.5 Non-payable items. Items listed in Annexure I (items for which coverage is not available in the policy) are not payable.

Section 4. Waiting periods

4.1 Migration credit. Where this policy is issued on migration from a group health policy of the Company, the period of continuous coverage under that group policy shall be credited against the waiting periods of this policy, up to the sum insured held under the group policy.

Section 5. Claims

5.1 Cashless. Cashless treatment is available at network Hospitals on pre-authorisation by the Company or its TPA.

5.2 Multiple policies. If the Insured Person holds more than one health policy, the Insured Person may choose the policy under which the claim is to be settled first; the balance may be claimed under the other policies.

5.3 Co-payment. Each admissible claim in respect of an Insured Person aged 61 years or above shall be subject to a co-payment of 20% of the admissible claim amount, which shall be borne by the Insured Person.

5.4 Grievances. The Insured Person may write to the Grievance Redressal Officer of the Company and, if not satisfied, approach the Insurance Ombudsman.
