2. Who is covered

2.1 You (the employee), your spouse, up to two children up to age 25, and your dependent parents or parents-in-law (one set, chosen at enrolment).

2.2 Dependants are added at joining or within 30 days of marriage or childbirth. Parents can be added only at joining or at policy renewal.

3. Sum insured

3.1 ₹5,00,000 per policy year, family floater. The sum insured is shared by all covered members of your family (employee, spouse, children and parents). There is no separate cover for parents.

3.2 Corporate buffer: Acme holds a buffer of ₹25,00,000 for the whole company for critical illness after a family's sum insured is used up, released at HR's discretion.

Your enrolled members for 2026–27: Rahul Sharma (employee, 34), Priya Sharma (spouse, 32), Anaya Sharma (daughter, 5), Suresh Sharma (father, 62), Kamala Sharma (mother, 58).

4. Room rent and ICU

4.1 Room rent limit: 1% of the sum insured per day (₹5,000 per day) for a normal room, including boarding and nursing.

4.2 ICU / ICCU: up to 2% of the sum insured per day (₹10,000 per day).

4.3 Proportionate deduction is waived under this group policy: if you choose a room above the limit, you pay only the extra room rent; other charges are not reduced.

5. Waiting periods and pre-existing diseases

5.1 Pre-existing diseases are covered from day one. The 30-day initial waiting period and the specific-disease waiting periods are waived for all members.

6. Maternity

6.1 Maternity: up to ₹50,000 for a normal delivery or caesarean section, within the family floater. Newborn covered from day one.

7. Co-payment

7.1 Nil co-payment for all members, including parents, under this group policy.

9. When you leave Acme

9.1 Cover for you and all your dependants ends on your last working day with Acme. There is no grace period. To keep cover, each covered member may migrate to an individual or family floater policy with Suraksha Health Insurance Ltd (SAMPLE). The migration request, with the migration form, must reach the insurer no later than 15 days before your last working day. Requests received after that are treated as fresh proposals, with medical underwriting and fresh waiting periods.

9.2 On migration, credit is given for the time each member has been continuously covered under this group policy, up to the sum insured held here (₹5,00,000), against waiting periods for pre-existing diseases and specific diseases.

9.3 The migrated policy is a retail policy: you pay its premium, and its own terms apply. In particular, room rent limits, proportionate deduction and co-payment for older members may apply even though they are waived under this group policy.

Leaving soon? Talk to the People Team at least 30 days before your last day so the migration form can be filed in time. Contact: benefits@acme-sample.example

10. Important notes

10.1 This summary does not replace the policy document. Exclusions and definitions are as per the group policy and the insurer's standard wording.

10.2 Acme may change the group cover at renewal on 1 April each year.
