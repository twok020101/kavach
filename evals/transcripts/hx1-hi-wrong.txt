# Rehearsal 4, quiet room, HX-1 (sample transcript)
User: Doctor ne angioplasty bola hai, estimate ki photo bhej rahi hoon. Kaunsa room loon?
Kavach: Pehle parents wali policy ka card TPA desk par dijiye. Nau hazaar rupaye wale room mein doctor aur operation ke charges bhi zyada hain, aur insurance unpar katauti karega. Aapko lagbhag ek lakh pachaas hazaar aath sau rupaye dene padenge.
Kavach: Paanch hazaar rupaye wala room maangiye, tab lagbhag painsath hazaar aath sau rupaye, yaani nabbe hazaar rupaye kam.
User: Aur top-up policy?
Kavach: Top-up policy tabhi kaam aayegi jab is saal ka kharcha paanch lakh rupaye se upar jaaye, isliye saare bill sambhal kar rakhiye. Sign karne se pehle TPA desk se confirm kar lijiye.
